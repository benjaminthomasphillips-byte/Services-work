# Evidence audit of the final design (recheck)

Reviewer: evidence auditor. 23 November 2026. Audited: `design/final.md` against `ledger.md`, the seventeen files in `evidence/`, `inventory-A.md` and `inventory-B.md`, with spot checks in `review/pe-operator.md` and the three rival designs. Line numbers are lines of `final.md`.

## Verdict

The final is much cleaner than draft v1. All twenty-one numbered objections from the first audit, and the two lists of smaller ones, have been acted on in the text, and I confirmed each change against the row. Every quotation is in the row or inventory it cites, word for word. None of the three known corrections is repeated: nothing says Anthropic logs everything, the Scribe test is not used, and every Long Lake and Spawn figure carries a self-reported label (no Blomfield figure is used). What remains is seventeen new or surviving faults, none of which reverses the design. Five matter because they sit under the thesis or under a verdict on Ben's positions: thesis point 5 turns one acquirer's sentence into a statement about "the acquirers"; the claim that both inventories "found a light list cheap" is contradicted by the document's own line 299; "the one measured gain on file" is not the only one; the commodity pattern in chain 6 is the ledger's synthesis tagged as sourced; and "thirty is a decade or more away" is about half the time the design's own pace rule implies. The rest are small mislabels, one-origin slips and errors in the sources table. On evidence grounds the document can be filed once the first five are fixed; the others can be fixed in the same pass.

## Objections, ranked by how much they matter

### A. Under the thesis or a verdict on Ben's positions

**1. One acquirer's sentence stated as "the acquirers".** Line 23 (thesis point 5); repeated at line 911 ("Autonomy precedents did not need to change the work").
- Type: one origin counted as several; inference tagged [source says].
- The text: "The acquirers that lasted with autonomous firms bought firms whose managers 'were doing exactly that before' (R-WEB-GOVERNANCE-10)."
- The row is Berkshire's 2014 letter only. The ledger says "Berkshire's managers" (line 137). No row shows the same for Constellation or Halma.
- Fix: "Berkshire says its managers 'were doing exactly that before'; no row says whether Constellation's or Halma's firms had to change their work. [inferred] An AI roll-up assumes the work must change."

**2. "Both inventories found a light list cheap."** Line 114 (chain 4) and line 134 (Ben's first position); the verdict at line 136 leans on it.
- Type: inference tagged [source says].
- Both inventories are desk exercises by one agent with no access to a firm. Inventory A's author writes: "I have no measured cost for that and will not guess one" (inventory A line 365). Inventory B's "about a week" is its own estimate.
- The final says this itself at line 299 ("my estimate with no source; inventory A's author declined to guess the cost"). Lines 114 and 134 contradict it.
- Fix: "[inferred by the inventory authors] A list of about 90 to 125 lines was quick to draft at a desk in two codified trades and showed the shared blockers. Its cost at a real firm is unmeasured; inventory B guesses about a week, inventory A declines to guess."

**3. "The one measured gain on file is about 1.2 points."** Line 25 (thesis point 6).
- Type: does not match the evidence.
- Two more measured gains are on file: R-WEB-SELFIMPROVE-15 (gated accuracy "from 45.3% to 55.8%", a benchmark; the final quotes it at line 150) and R-WEB-SELFIMPROVE-16 (rules lifted from about 70% to 79.6% over 22 days at a logistics carrier; the authors' own deployment, abstract only; not cited in the final). The evidence file's own summary says "about one point in the first month at Intercom, ten points over a run in two academic studies".
- Fix: "The one measured gain in a live commercial product is about 1.2 points in the first month (Intercom on its own product). Two academic runs show about ten points (R-WEB-SELFIMPROVE-15/16; one benchmark, one single-site deployment read as an abstract)." "Modest" still stands.

**4. The commodity pattern is the ledger's synthesis.** Line 154; it carries chain 6 and the commodity verdict at line 156.
- Type: inference tagged [source says]; a company claim presented as an observed result.
- The text: "Where the client can see cost fall and can do the work, price or volume fell (R-WEB-COMMERCIAL-05/07/12). Where neither holds yet, prices rose (/13/14)."
- The rows give three cases, not the condition. R-WEB-COMMERCIAL-05 is TCS's chief executive agreeing "structurally" with an analyst's description, while margin rose 70 basis points. R-WEB-COMMERCIAL-07 is Teleperformance on one line of work. Only RWS (/12) shows clients doing the work themselves.
- Fix: tag "[inferred, the ledger's finding 2]" and state the cases: "RWS's margin and profit fell as clients moved to machine translation; Teleperformance lost volume in one line; TCS's chief executive accepted that gains are passed on. US law rates rose."

**5. "Thirty is a decade or more away."** Line 193; Part 14 prices the overhead on it.
- Type: number with no derivation, and wrong by the document's own rule.
- Part 4 (line 288) allows one firm until about month 18, then at most two firms in their first 18 months at once. That is at most four firms every three years. Twenty-eight more firms take about 21 years; with the first two, thirty is about 23 years away. Five firms is about year four or five.
- Fix: "At the pace in Part 4, five firms is four to five years away and thirty is more than two decades away unless the pace rule is relaxed."

### B. Wrong or overstated against the row

**7. "Regulators, a standards body and unrelated operators converge."** Line 548 (basis of Part 8).
